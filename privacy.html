2a. Introduction videos

Teachers may upload or record a short introduction video. That video is personal data: it shows a face and records a voice. It is stored in a private object-storage bucket at DigitalOcean (Amsterdam), converted to a standard format, and shown publicly only when the profile is approved, publication consent has been given, and the video has been processed. Reviewers can watch it before any decision. At upload, the teacher must confirm that the video is their own and that they have the right to share it. Replacing a video deletes the previous file; withdrawing the application or deleting the account removes the video and its stored files.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the service — create and secure your account, publish an approved profile, let clients contact professionals, and respond to enquiries. Legal basis: performance of a contract, or steps taken at your request before a contract.
  • To review applications — a human reviewer checks submitted material before anything is published. Legal basis: legitimate interests in keeping the directory trustworthy, and your consent to review where documents are supplied for verification.
  • To prevent abuse — a bot check on sign-up, rate limits, and logging of failed authentication. Legal basis: legitimate interests in protecting the service and its users.
  • To send service email — verification links, password resets, and notices about your account. Legal basis: performance of a contract. These are not marketing messages.
  • To meet legal obligations — responding to lawful requests and keeping records where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

4. Cookies and browser storage

We use no advertising or analytics cookies. What exists is limited to:

  • Sign-in state — a session token kept by the browser so you stay signed in.
  • Preferences — your language choice and saved instructors, stored locally in your browser.
  • Bot check — Cloudflare Turnstile may set a cookie or use similar storage to distinguish people from automated sign-ups.

You can clear these at any time through your browser settings; clearing the session token signs you out, and clearing preferences resets the language to English.

6. How long we keep it

  • Accounts — 24 months after your last activity, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Rejected applications — 12 months after the decision, so a later re-application can be assessed consistently.
  • Published profiles — while published, and for 12 months after you ask us to remove them, to prevent duplicate or disputed listings.
  • Enquiries — 24 months from receipt.
  • Encrypted database backups — rolling 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Introduction videos — while the profile is published; deleted when the teacher withdraws, when the account is deleted, or when a replacement finishes processing.

We may keep records longer where the law requires, or where needed to establish or defend a legal claim.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, where we have no overriding obligation to keep it;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent you previously gave, without affecting processing already carried out.

Write to tareq@jeninmediahouse.com. We answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity first. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the data-protection authority where you live — in Morocco, the CNDP (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel); in the EU or UK, your national authority.

8. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to the administration area is restricted to named accounts, database access is limited to the roles that need it, uploaded documents are scanned before review, and database backups are encrypted before they leave the server. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

9. Automated decisions

The bot check on sign-up is automated and refuses submissions that fail it; you can contact us if you believe you were refused in error. Decisions about publishing a professional profile are made by a human reviewer, not automatically.

10. Children

Marocora is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

11. Changes

We will publish any change on this page and update the version and date above. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell you by email or with a notice on the site before it takes effect.

12. Language

This policy is published in English and Arabic. The English version governs if they differ, except where mandatory law gives you stronger rights.

3. لماذا نستخدم البيانات

  • لتقديم الخدمة — إنشاء حسابك وتأمينه، ونشر الملف بعد الموافقة، وتمكين العملاء من التواصل مع المهنيين، والرد على الاستفسارات. الأساس: تنفيذ عقد أو خطوات تمهيدية بطلب منك.
  • لمراجعة الطلبات — يراجع شخص بشري المواد المقدَّمة قبل النشر. الأساس: مصلحة مشروعة في موثوقية الدليل، وموافقتك على المراجعة عند تقديم وثائق للتحقق.
  • لمنع إساءة الاستخدام — فحص الروبوت عند التسجيل، وحدود المعدل، وتسجيل محاولات الدخول الفاشلة. الأساس: مصلحة مشروعة في حماية الخدمة والمستخدمين.
  • لإرسال بريد الخدمة — روابط التحقق، وإعادة تعيين كلمة المرور، وإشعارات الحساب. الأساس: تنفيذ عقد. وهذه ليست رسائل تسويقية.
  • للالتزام القانوني — الاستجابة للطلبات المشروعة وحفظ السجلات حيث يوجب القانون.

لا نبيع البيانات الشخصية، ولا نشاركها لأغراض إعلانية.
