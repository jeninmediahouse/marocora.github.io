What these numbers mean

These are current status totals, not new applications today. Permanent test applications, including Tanya, are excluded by the existing production summary. Approval does not mean a public profile, booking or paid service.

Client requests, email delivery totals, revenue and Formspree usage are not included in these vendor counts. The client queue may contain controlled test receipts, so its raw count must not be reported as real customer demand.
