Teaching: 70% at the start, 80% once you are established

Teaching sits at the top of the Marocora scale. You begin at 70% of every completed teaching hour, and your share rises in steps as you complete more hours. At 400 completed teaching hours you reach 80%, and that 80% is permanent — it does not drop back.

Only completed teaching hours count toward your progression.

First 20 completed hours
70%

You keep 70% of what the client pays for each completed hour.

Hours 21 – 399
70% → 80%

Your share increases at fixed milestones as your completed hours accumulate.

400 completed hours and beyond
80%

You keep 80% for good, on every completed teaching hour after that.

Already completed hours are never recalculated downward.

Other services: a straightforward 70/30 split

Translation, interpreting, transportation and every other professional category on Marocora work on one flat split, from your first job onward. There are no tiers, no probation period and no hidden deductions.

Category You keep Marocora keeps
Teaching and tutoring 70% → 80% 30% → 20%
Translation and interpreting 70% 30%
Transportation and local services 70% 30%
All other professional services 70% 30%

You set the price of your own service. Marocora's share is taken from the amount the client pays, not added on top of it — so the price a client sees is the price they pay.

What the Marocora share covers

The 20% or 30% is not a listing fee. It is what keeps the marketplace running and brings clients to your profile.

Included in Marocora's share

  • Processing client payments and handling the transaction
  • Managing bookings, requests and scheduling
  • Reviewing provider profiles before they are published
  • Client support when something goes wrong
  • Hosting, maintenance and ongoing development of the platform
  • Bringing new clients to the marketplace

What you are never charged for

  • Creating your profile
  • Being listed or remaining listed
  • Updating your experience, services or rates
  • Receiving a booking request
  • Monthly or annual subscription
  • Anything at all before a service is completed

When and how you get paid

Earnings become payable once the booked service has actually been completed — not when it is booked, and not before the client has received what they paid for.

Questions providers ask

If something is missing here, email us and it will be added.

Do I set my own prices?

Yes. You decide what your lesson, translation, trip or service costs. Marocora's share is taken from that price, so the client pays exactly what you quoted.

What counts as a completed teaching hour?

An hour of teaching that has actually taken place and been delivered through Marocora. Only completed hours move you up the progression toward 80%.

If I teach and also translate, which split applies?

Each service is paid under its own category. Teaching follows the 70% → 80% progression, and translation or any other service is paid at 70/30. One does not affect the other.

Can my percentage go down later?

No. Once you reach 80% at 400 completed teaching hours, you keep 80% permanently. Changes to the published terms would apply only to providers joining afterwards, and would be posted here and emailed to you first.

What happens if a client cancels or asks for a refund?

The cancellation and refund rules will be published with the booking terms before bookings open. Where work has already been delivered, the provider is paid for it.

Who handles taxes?

Each provider is responsible for reporting their own earnings. Marocora keeps a record of completed services and amounts on the platform so you have a clear history to work from.

Is there a minimum amount I have to earn before I get paid?

The final payout terms, including any minimum threshold, will be published here before customer bookings open. If a minimum is set, it will be low enough that normal activity clears it quickly.
