GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin

Which languages do you translate?

Arabic, Chinese (Mandarin), English, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Turkish.

Can we see a sample before we order?

Yes. We translate a 250-word sample of your document free of charge. Your first order then has a 10% discount.

How do we pay for a translation?

You pay 50% when you confirm the quote, and the other 50% when we deliver the translation.

How long does a translation take?

A translator translates about 2,000 words a day. A 10,000-word report takes about five working days. Your quote gives you the delivery date.
